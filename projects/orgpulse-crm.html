KPI Formula Notes

Tracking is calculated as actual ÷ target for growth KPIs. For limit-based KPIs such as cost, response time, escalations, overdue AR, open roles, and new bugs, tracking is calculated as target ÷ actual so lower values score better.

  • Daily view shows whether the department needs action today.
  • Weekly view shows whether the department can finish the current week on target.
  • Monthly and quarterly views show strategic progress and recovery gaps.
  • Values are structured from the uploaded CRM hub sample and should be connected to actual CRM / finance / analytics sources before live use.
